About the Firm

A tradition of judgment, earned over decades

Founded in 1988, Whitmore Legal has grown from a two-partner practice into one of the region's most respected full-service firms — without ever losing the personal attention that defined its beginnings. We measure our work not in billable hours, but in the confidence our clients feel when they hand us the matters that keep them awake at night.

Our attorneys combine the resources of a large firm with the responsiveness of a boutique. Every engagement is led by a senior lawyer, informed by rigorous preparation, and guided by a single principle: your interests come first.

  • Senior-led representation on every matter, start to finish.
  • Transparent counsel and fee structures — no surprises.
  • Discretion and confidentiality held to the highest standard.
